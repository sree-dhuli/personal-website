The Tanpura

A tanpura is the four-string drone behind every Carnatic performance. Its strings are plucked in order, over and over: P̣a, Sa, Sa, Ṣa. A dot under a swara marks the lower octave. Pluck a string or sweep across them.

The Swaras (musical notes)

Carnatic music has seven swaras, Sa Ri Ga Ma Pa Da Ni: Sa and Pa never change, the other five each have two or three variants, and every raga (a melodic framework) picks its own set from the 12 positions. Play them over the drone.
